Tuto, from the Latin in tuto (in safety), is a castle keep for your receipts, warranties, and letters. Nothing leaves the walls. Drop in photos and PDFs, or snap them on your phone and send them across the drawbridge of your home Wi-Fi to the laptop. Tesseract reads them, SQLite files them away, and search lights the words like torches. No cloud, no account, no gate left open. Built for Hackyard Yard #6.
