Tuto, from the Latin in tuto (in safety), is a Fort Knox for receipts, warranties, and letters that stay at home. Drop in photos and PDFs. It reads them on your machine with Tesseract, stores them with a SQLite full-text index in the app data folder, and searches instantly with highlighted snippets. Airplane mode changes nothing: nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Built for Hackyard Yard #6.
